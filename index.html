How this estimate works

This prototype uses a transparent rule-based score, informed by the themes in the Youth Opportunity, Migration, and Return Intent Survey (Nepal). It is not a trained or validated AI model. The available findings summarize 72 responses; they do not show how each person's answers relate to their migration intention.

Job, education and career satisfaction each contribute 0–4 points (lower satisfaction adds more points). Your main goal contributes 0–4, family abroad contributes 0–1, and preparation contributes 0–4. A total of 0–6 leans toward staying, 7–13 is uncertain, and 14–21 leans toward leaving. These weights and thresholds are design choices, not relationships learned from the survey. No personal probability or model accuracy is claimed.

The sample is concentrated among students and respondents from Bagmati and Koshi. Aggregate counts were combined from two groups of 36, with some counts reconstructed from rounded percentages. The survey's 63.9% is a sample statistic, not your personal chance of leaving. Family abroad is context and does not establish causation. Age, gender, income and region are not used to score you.
